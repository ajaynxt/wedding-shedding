About Wedding Shedding & Founder Sonu Kumar

Operational since 2015 with over a decade of excellence in the visual storytelling craft, Wedding Shedding was founded by visual artist and lead cinematographer Sonu Kumar on a single passionate conviction: that wedding photography should never feel like an awkward staged photoshoot, but an authentic celebration of human emotion.

Headquartered in our private studio in Burari, Delhi (Gali NO 01, near Lance Ford School, Sunil Colony, Parshuram Enclave), Sonu Kumar directs a dedicated in-house collective of 10–12 visual artists, colorists, and licensed aerial drone pilots — ensuring every frame is captured with personal devotion, never outsourced to third-party freelancers.

Armed with top-tier 4K cinema gear, color-calibrated master grading suites, and drone aerial cameras, our team travels extensively across Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Udaipur, and royal destination venues throughout India. Our uncompromised dedication is validated by a 4.9★ rating on WeddingWire and consecutive WeddingWire Industry Awards in 2024 & 2025.

Booking Process & Policies

We maintain absolute transparency with our couples so you can focus entirely on enjoying your big day.

1. Date Reservation

A 30% advance deposit is required at the time of signing to officially block your wedding dates and dedicate our in-house visual crew exclusively to your celebration.

2. Cancellation Policy

Our reservation deposit is non-refundable as we decline all other inquiries for your booked dates to guarantee undivided commitment.

3. Flexible Payment Modes

We accept Cash Payments (at studio or shoot site), UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm), Net Banking (NEFT / RTGS), Cards, and Cheques. Official printed receipts and signed GST invoices are provided for all transactions.
