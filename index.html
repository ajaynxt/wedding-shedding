About Wedding Shedding

Operational since 2015 with over a decade of excellence in the visual storytelling craft, Wedding Shedding is a premier luxury wedding photography and cinematography studio.

Headquartered in Pandav Nagar, East Delhi, our team of 10–12 dedicated in-house visual artists specializes in capturing spontaneous, unfiltered emotions — the nervous whispers before the bridal entry, celebratory laughter during haldi splashes, and sacred tears during the vidaai.

Armed with 4K cinema systems, drone aerial cameras, and personalized editing suites, we travel extensively across Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, and destination wedding venues throughout India.

Booking Process & Policies

We maintain absolute transparency with our couples so you can focus entirely on enjoying your big day.

1. Date Reservation

A 30% advance deposit is required at the time of signing to officially block your wedding dates and dedicate our in-house visual crew exclusively to your celebration.

2. Cancellation Policy

Our reservation deposit is non-refundable as we decline all other inquiries for your booked dates to guarantee undivided commitment.

★ Cash & Online Accepted

3. Flexible Payment Modes (Cash Accepted)

We accept Cash Payments (at studio or shoot site), UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm), Net Banking (NEFT / RTGS), Cards, and Cheques. Official printed receipts and signed GST invoices are provided for all transactions.
