ABOUT WEDDING SHEDDING & SONU KUMAR

Founded by Sonu Kumar, Wedding Shedding was born out of a genuine passion for capturing the magic, sacred rituals, and unfiltered joy of Indian weddings. Based in Delhi NCR, we believe that the most powerful wedding photographs are not posed or staged, but the unscripted glances, happy tears during pheras, and the vibrant laughter of Haldi and Sangeet.

With over 11+ years of dedicated wedding photography experience and 350+ couples documented across Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, and destination venues across India, our team combines fine-art candid photography with breathtaking 4K wedding cinema — preserving every heartfelt ritual in timeless, museum-grade photobooks.
