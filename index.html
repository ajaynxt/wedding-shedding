ABOUT SONU KUMAR

My journey began in the world of films. After studying filmmaking, I worked as an assistant director on television commercials and collaborated with some of the finest cinematographers in the industry. Over time, my love for still imagery led me to photography — where I found the perfect blend of storytelling, light and emotion.

With over 14 years of experience and 350+ weddings photographed across India and abroad, I continue to explore diverse genres — from weddings and architecture to aerials, commercials and personal projects.
