Pre-wedding shoot FAQs

How much does a pre-wedding shoot cost?

Request a tailored quote with your location, shoot duration and preferred deliverables. Venue charges and travel may be additional.

Can we book photos and a pre-wedding film together?

Yes, enquire about combined photography and film coverage and confirm the final deliverables with the studio.

Can we choose a location outside Delhi?

Destination enquiries are welcome. Crew availability, travel and venue permissions need confirmation before booking.

Do we need permission for an outdoor shoot?

Some public and private locations require permission or fees. Drone filming is subject to applicable restrictions and approvals.
